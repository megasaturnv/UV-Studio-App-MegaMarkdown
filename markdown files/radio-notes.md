# Radio Notes

Example field notes for the **UV-K1 / UV-K5 v3** with the Fusion firmware.

## Everyday controls

- Press `Menu` to open the menu.
- Press `Exit` to exit the current screen.
- Hold `F` to lock or unlock the keypad.
- Functions under `F` key.
> `F` then `5` opens the RF spectrum.
> `F` then `7` opens the Apps menu.

## Good operating habits

1. Listen before you transmit.
2. Use the lowest power that reaches the other station.
3. Say your callsign clearly and slowly.
4. Keep transmissions short.

## PMR Channels
Here is the first 3 PMR channels and their frequencies in a table

| No. | Freq |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 446.00625 |
| 2 | 446.01875 |
| 3 | 446.03125 |

A saved channel remembers its frequency, tone and power setting. Use `MENU` on a channel to edit it.

## Scanning

The scanner stops on a signal and resumes after a short delay. Lock out busy channels so the scan keeps moving.

## Serial port

The UV-K5 v3 requires a special serial cable that communicates through the side ports. The UV-K1 has a usb serial interface internally so only needs a USB-C cable. Set the terminal to 115200 baud, 8 data bits, no parity and one stop bit.

## Battery care

- Charge before the battery is completely flat.
- Do not fully charge.
- Do not leave the radio in direct sun.
- Store the battery at about half charge.
